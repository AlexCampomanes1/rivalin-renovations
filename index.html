Built on Craftsmanship & Trust

My renovation journey began during university, building decks and fences to pay for tuition. After moving to Calgary to expand my skills in home renovations, I returned to Winnipeg with a clear vision: build a company focused on meticulous workmanship and helping customers see their dream spaces come to life.

For 28 years, I've been transforming Winnipeg homes with the same dedication and attention to detail that started it all. Every project is a partnership where we listen, plan carefully, and execute with precision.
